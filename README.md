### 🔬 Engineering Research & Hardware

#### ⭐ [Master's Thesis] [Smart Classroom Platform](https://github.com/rabahdj2002/smart-classroom-pfe)
*An IoT platform that digitalises campus classrooms.*
* **The Solution:** classroom devices report occupancy, door status and RFID badge scans over MQTT to a Django control centre that manages sessions, attendance and reports.
* **Tech Stack:** ESP32, RFID, MQTT, Django, Django Channels (WebSockets), ReportLab.
* **Features:** live room status, timetable-based teacher access, automatic attendance, PDF reports by email.
* 📄 **[Read Thesis (PDF)](https://rabahdj2002.github.io/master%27s%20thesis%20en.pdf)**

#### 👷 [Bachelor's Project] [Project HALO: Smart Safety Helmet](https://github.com/rabahdj2002/smart-Helmet) 
*An IoT-enabled hard hat for construction safety, achieving **96.7% accuracy** in fall detection.*
* **Tech Stack:** ESP32, MPU6050 (6-axis Gyro/Accel), GPS (NEO-6M), MQ135 Gas Sensor.
* **Impact:** Implemented a custom fall detection algorithm ($Amp \le 2g$ drop followed by $>12g$ impact) and a real-time supervisor dashboard with live incident alerts over MQTT.
* 📄 **[Read Full Thesis (PDF)](https://rabahdj2002.github.io/Memoire.pdf)**
